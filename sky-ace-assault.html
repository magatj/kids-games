Mission Brief

Fly through a stormfront, shred attack waves, collect upgrades, and bring down the flagship. The pacing is tuned to feel close to a Raiden-style arcade run without copying the original game’s assets or layout.

Move Arrow keys or WASD. You can also drag the jet with mouse or touch.
Fire Your cannon auto-fires. Power pickups add spread shots and side missiles.
Bomb Press B or tap Bomb to clear bullets and deal heavy screen damage.
Pause Press P or tap Pause any time without losing your run.
